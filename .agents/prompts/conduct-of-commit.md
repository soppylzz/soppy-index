# Commits

Commit only when authorized. Follow the repository's commitlint configuration: English `type: summary`, no scope, with `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `refactor`, or `chore`. Use a concise imperative summary.

After a blank line, write the body as a bullet list describing the commit's changes. Add motivation or validation when useful; avoid repeating the title or narrating abandoned attempts. Do not fabricate attribution.

Inspect status, existing staging, diffs, and recent messages. Stage one coherent change with explicit paths or hunks; preserve unrelated work. Review the staged diff, run relevant checks, and respect hooks. Report the resulting hash, subject, checks, and remaining changes. Commit authorization does not authorize push, amend, or history rewrites.
